For a gradeable photo

  • Centre the optic disc and macula in the frame.
  • Avoid eyelashes, glare and dark corners.
  • Refocus if the vessels look soft.

How to read the result

  • 0 · No apparent DR
  • 1 · Mild NPDR
  • 2 · Moderate NPDR — refer
  • 3 · Severe NPDR — refer
  • 4 · Proliferative DR — refer
